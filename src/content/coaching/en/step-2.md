# What this step is for

Narrow the problem down with data before anyone asks why it happens. Where exactly, in what form, how often — so the team attacks the slice that actually matters instead of the whole problem at once.

## What good looks like

At least one data-based entry — a Pareto, a trend chart, a check sheet, a stratification table — that points at a specific point of cause: one machine, one shift, one part number, one failure mode. Not "the whole line," a specific slice of it.

## Common failure modes

- Skipping straight to root cause. Fishbone and 5-Why belong in Step 4, not here — this step is about *where*, not *why*.
- Stratifying by opinion instead of data: "it's probably the night shift" without a shift-by-shift breakdown to back it up.
- No point of cause nominated — data was collected but nobody drew a conclusion from it.

## Worked example

A Pareto of the last 200 bracket rejects shows 68% are one weld-nugget-diameter failure, concentrated on station 3, second shift. The point of cause: station 3's welder settings, second shift.
