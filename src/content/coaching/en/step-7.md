# What this step is for

Check whether the target from Step 3 was actually met, and whether the countermeasure ran the way it was designed to — not just whether the number moved.

## What good looks like

A recorded result against the Step 3 target, plus a process confirmation: was the countermeasure actually executed as designed, on the floor, not just on paper? If the target wasn't met, this step opens a new round back at Step 4 rather than quietly extending the deadline.

## Common failure modes

- Recording the result but leaving process confirmation blank — a good number with no evidence the countermeasure is what caused it.
- Treating a missed target as a reason to loosen the target instead of a reason to dig deeper at Step 4.
- Checking once right after implementation and never again — a countermeasure that erodes over three months looks fine on day one.

## Worked example

Target was 1.0% by week 34; actual result at week 34 is 1.3%. Process confirmation: the poka-yoke is active and logging correctly. Since the target wasn't met despite correct execution, a new round opens at Step 4 — the calibration fix wasn't the whole story.
